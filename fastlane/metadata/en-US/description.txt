Where were you a year ago today?

Photos On This Day brings back the photos you took on today's date in every past year, so each day starts with a little time travel. No digging through thousands of pictures and nothing to set up: just today, years ago.

YOUR MEMORIES ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
• Widgets in every size show a photo from this day and how many years ago you took it
• The Years widget lines up one photo from each past year
• Lock Screen widgets count today's memories
• Choose how often the photos change, or shuffle to a new one with a tap

RELIVE THE WHOLE DAY
• Every photo and video from today's date, grouped by year
• A full-screen viewer that plays Live Photos and videos, with sharp pinch-to-zoom
• A map of where each photo was taken
• Sit back and watch the day as a slideshow
• On a day without photos, jump to nearby days that have some

SHARE THE DAY
• Turn the day into a post, a story or a slideshow video, with the best photos from each year picked for you

MADE TO FIT IN
• Includes photos friends and family share with you in iCloud
• Ask Siri for today's photos, a year's photos or a random memory, or use them in Shortcuts
• Every few months, if you haven't looked in a while, a gentle reminder on a day full of photos

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos never leave your device. No account, no tracking, no data collected. No ads, ever.
